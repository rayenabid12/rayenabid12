# Rectify — Behance case study

22 panels, 2800 px wide (1400 px at 2x), ready to upload to Behance in this order: `png/01-hero.jpg` → `png/17-closing.jpg`.

- `panels.js` renders the new panels. Run `node panels.js` (or `node panels.js 09` to redo one panel).
- Panels whose names have a letter (`12b-app`, `15c-stationery`…) come from `../mockups/`.
- `rectify-case-study.html` is the long-scroll preview page.

## New ideas in this round

- **Specs, the mascot.** The symbol's pupils change shape: curious, happy, wow, thinking, wink, proud.
- **The Lens.** A photo frame cut from the logo's glasses.
- **Learning path card.** A stats card (subject, quiz score, streak) for social posts and the app.
- **Tagline:** Learn. Laugh. Look closer.
