# Rectify — Behance case study

33 panels, 2800 px wide (1400 px at 2x), ready to upload to Behance in this order: `png/01-hero.jpg` → `png/17-closing.jpg`.

- `panels.js` renders the new panels. Run `node panels.js` (or `node panels.js 09` to redo one panel).
- Panels whose names have a letter (`12b-app`, `15c-stationery`…) come from `../mockups/`.
- `rectify-case-study.html` is the long-scroll preview page.

## Concept development (round 3)

- **Brand idea:** "Every kid is already curious. Rectify helps them look closer." It rests on three pillars: See clearly, Play seriously, Grow together.
- **Logo construction:** five steps (stem, bowl, bridge, lenses, spark) and proportions on a grid based on the stem width x: height 7x, bowl 4x, lens 1x × 0.6x, leg at 41°.
- **Logo motion:** a 1.4 s blink and smile sequence for the app loader and video end cards.
- **Iconography:** drawn with the logo's stroke, with one orange detail per icon.
- **Photography:** four rules (eye level, mid-discovery, warm light with one orange note, close enough to see), plus a list of things to avoid.
- **Grading recipe,** a **tritone** campaign treatment (Ink, Orange, Cream) and **the Zoom** (dimmed scene, one magnified detail).
- **Moodboard and layout system:** "Corner. Lens. Line."

## New ideas in this round

- **Specs, the mascot.** The symbol's pupils change shape: curious, happy, wow, thinking, wink, proud.
- **The Lens.** A photo frame cut from the logo's glasses.
- **Learning path card.** A stats card (subject, quiz score, streak) for social posts and the app.
- **Tagline:** Learn. Laugh. Look closer.
