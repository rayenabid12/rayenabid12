## Project title
Muscle Stock — Brand Identity

## Description (paste into the project description)
Muscle Stock is a Tunisian sports nutrition brand serving fitness lovers, athletes and bodybuilders.
This project is a full refresh of the 2024 brand guidelines: same DNA — the bodybuilder mascot,
Crimson Red and black — rebuilt into a stricter, more usable system.

What's inside:
• Brand platform: manifesto, vision, mission, five values, audiences, tone of voice
• New tagline: "Strength, in stock."
• Logo system in four formats: emblem, horizontal lockup, round symbol, face monogram
• Clear space, minimum sizes and misuse rules
• Palette with a new Bone neutral and colour-coded product lines (Performance, Recovery, Essentials)
• Typography: Sakana (wordmark), Barlow Condensed (headings), Roboto (text)
• Graphic language built on a single 14° cut, plus a red duotone photo treatment
• Photography direction: mood, five-shot list, shooting rules and an AI prompt recipe
• AI product photography and 6 Instagram post + 2 story templates
• Applications: supplement packaging, shaker and bottle, apparel, business and loyalty cards,
  storefront signage, Instagram and a responsive online store
