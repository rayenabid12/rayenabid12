01

The brand

Most crypto “signal” groups sell hype. We built the opposite: a community where every call comes with an entry, a target, a stop and the reasoning behind it, priced so that any Tunisian can afford to learn.

We help Tunisians understand the market before they trade it.

Mission

Give Tunisians affordable, transparent trading signals and the education to use them, so they can protect their capital, avoid scams and grow with confidence.

Vision

Become the most trusted crypto resource in Tunisia: a lasting platform where beginners and experienced traders learn, share and navigate digital assets together.

Value · 1

Strategy

Every signal is a plan: entry zone, targets, stop loss and risk. The king piece in our mark stands for this.

Value · 2

Transparency

We publish wins and losses alike. Results are tracked in public channels, never cherry-picked.

Value · 3

Education

We explain the why behind each call, so members become traders rather than followers.

Value · 4

Community

Tunisians helping Tunisians, in Arabic, French and English. Supportive, never elitist.

Who we talk to

Two audiences, one tone: clear enough for the first trade, precise enough for the hundredth.

Newcomers
18–30, curious about crypto, burned or nearly burned by a “guaranteed profit” group. Need trust and plain language.
Active traders
Already trading spot or futures. Want clean, fast, well-reasoned setups and a serious community.
Languages
English for brand assets · French and Tunisian Arabic in community content · Modern Standard Arabic for formal material.

Logo

Two mirrored C’s, for Crypto and Signals, meet in a single stem crowned by the chess king. Read together, the form also suggests an infinity loop: markets cycle, strategy endures.

TunisiaCrypto Signals
Primary lockup · horizontal

Anatomy

Each part of the mark carries meaning. Use these descriptions when presenting the brand.

1King pieceStrategy and leadership. Smart guidance for moving through the market.
2Mirrored C’sThe cyclical market. Adapting to trends instead of fighting them.
3Shared stemCrypto and Signals joined into one: information that works together.
4Open gatewayThe cut in each C: an opening to opportunity, held with precision.

Construction

The mark is built from two identical rings. Each ring’s stroke equals 38% of its outer radius, and the rings sit exactly one stroke-width apart so their inner edges share the central stem. The C openings align with the crown’s outer arm tips.

05

Graphic elements

A small kit, all derived from the mark: the ring pattern, the Crown Marker for charts, the signal card and a line-icon set. Together they make any post recognisably ours, even without the logo.

Ring pattern

Built from the lower arcs of the two C’s, repeated in offset rows. Use at 8–18% opacity behind content, in Mist on Night or Royal. Never behind body text at full strength.

Crown Marker

Our chart signature. The crown pins the entry point; dashed green lines mark targets, red marks the stop, and the entry zone is a soft Mist band. Price lines are always Mist, 3 px.

Signal card

The brand’s most-seen asset. Every call follows the same structure, in the same order, with the risk note on every card. Consistency is what makes members trust the format.

Taglines

  • Signals with a strategy. Primary
  • Trade with a plan.
  • Learn first. Then trade.
  • تداوَل بخطة.

We say

  • “ETH long setup. Entry 3,180–3,220, stop 3,090. Risk 1% of your account.”
  • “TP1 hit. Move your stop to entry and let the rest run.”
  • “This one stopped out at −1.2R. Here is what we missed.”
  • “New to futures? Start with the Academy before trading leverage.”

We never say

  • “GUARANTEED 10x!!! Don’t miss this 🚀🚀🚀”
  • “Easy money, no experience needed.”
  • “Only 3 VIP spots left, pay now.”
  • “We never lose.”

The risk line is part of the brand

Every signal, ad and results post carries this line, set in Mono at label size: “Not financial advice. Crypto is volatile. Only risk what you can afford to lose.” It protects members and it is the clearest proof that we are not another hype group.
