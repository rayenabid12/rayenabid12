Rebranding a premium parapharmacy in Nabeul, Tunisia — from a logo to a complete, calm and confident identity system.
Overview
A wellness sanctuary for the modern woman.
Para Elixea is a premium parapharmacy in Slimen, Nabeul. The owners wanted to stand apart from the clinical, crowded stores that dominate the market — and build a place women trust for expert advice and carefully selected care products.

The existing PE monogram was kept. Everything around it was redesigned into a coherent system: platform, concept, palette, typography, graphic language and every touchpoint.

Challenge → Insight → Idea
01
The challenge
Local parapharmacies are either cold and clinical — white walls, green crosses — or loud and cluttered with promotions. Neither feels like a place to take care of yourself.
02
The insight
The store itself already had the answer: its shelves sit inside soft arched niches. A space that protects and displays — like a sanctuary.
03
The idea
Design the whole brand like a place: less noise, more space, expert advice at the centre — built from three elements: the Arch, the Line and the Leaf.
Design concept
The Arch · The Line · The Leaf
The Arch
Taken from the store's niches. It frames products, faces and information — photo crops, packaging windows, signage.
The Line
The monogram's single, constant, round-ended stroke becomes the rule for every icon, divider and illustration.
The Leaf
The leaf-shaped "e" lifted from the symbol. Repeated, it becomes the signature pattern — quiet, botanical, ownable.
From space to shape
The store drew the identity.
The arched niches of the Slimen store were simplified into one graphic form — a rectangle topped with a perfect semicircle — used to "put things on display".
The mark
The original PE monogram — a P whose bowl interlaces with a leaf drawing the "e" — was redrawn as a clean vector and paired with a Gotham wordmark.
